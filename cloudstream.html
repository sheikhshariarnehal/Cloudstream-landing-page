Provider Repositories

CloudStream functions as a clean engine. Simply add your favorite community repository URL in the app settings to fetch index metadata and playback links.

1

Download and launch the CloudStream application on your device.

2

Navigate to Settings > Extensions > Add Repository.

3

Paste the extension repository link to immediately index media.
